 "We are analyzing the static disintegration images of three self-made gliclazide matrix tablet formulations with different HPMC ratios. "  
    "All images were captured under the same experimental conditions. "  
    "The dissolution medium is initially pH 1.2, and after 1 hour it is switched to pH 6.8. "  
    "The experiment was conducted at a controlled temperature of 38 °C to simulate a slightly elevated physiological condition. "  
    "The system is a flow-through cell with a flow rate of 1 mL/min. "  
    "The total observation period is 24 h. Images were captured continuously and later sampled into batches. "  
    "Each message you receive contains multiple images representing a time window (e.g., 0–6 h, 6–12 h, 12–18 h, 18–24 h). "  
    "Treat the images within the same window as observations from that time range rather than a single exact time point. "  
    "These three formulations are all gliclazide tablets, and their main difference lies in the ratio between HPMC K4M and HPMC 100lv. "  
    "The first formulation is the low-viscosity formulation. In this formulation, gliclazide is 4.505, silicon dioxide is 0.049, magnesium stearate is 0.174, microcrystalline cellulose is 7.503, lactose is 7.504, HPMC K4M is 1.058, and HPMC 100lv is 4.248. "  
    "The second formulation is the standard formulation, which is also the medium-viscosity formulation. In this formulation, gliclazide is 4.493, silicon dioxide is 0.049, magnesium stearate is 0.162, microcrystalline cellulose is 7.503, lactose is 7.506, HPMC K4M is 2.504, and HPMC 100lv is 2.808. "  
    "The third formulation is the high-viscosity formulation. In this formulation, gliclazide is 4.500, silicon dioxide is 0.049, magnesium stearate is 0.162, microcrystalline cellulose is 7.495, lactose is 7.504, HPMC K4M is 4.252, and HPMC 100lv is 1.063. "  
    "Our optimization goal is to adjust the ratio between HPMC K4M and HPMC 100lv so that the 24-hour release amount of the standard-viscosity gliclazide tablet is reduced to 90% of its original level." 
    "Please treat all images as coming from these three related gliclazide formulations, and use this background information when interpreting the disintegration behavior and describing the eight dimensions of disintegration. "
